> category Etsy removes.

> **2. Never position it as advice.** Not "how to find love", not "attract the right
> partner", nothing that reads as a psychological claim. It is an organizing and
> reflection tool. That framing is honest, it's safe, and it genuinely converts better —
> this buyer is sick of being sold a method.
