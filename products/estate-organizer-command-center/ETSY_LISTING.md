## 5. PRICING & RANK BOOSTERS

> ⚠ **This listing needs more care than any other in the shop. Two hard rules:**
>
> **1. Never imply legal validity.** Do not write "will kit", "legal documents",
> "legally binding", "attorney-approved" or "estate plan" anywhere in the listing. Use
> **organizer**, **checklist**, **record**. Etsy does not pull organizing tools; it does
> pull unlicensed legal-document sales, and so do several state bars. The disclaimer above
> is not decoration — keep it in the listing body.
>
> **2. No services.** No setup help, consultations, **coaching or mentoring**, document
> review, or "free updates / lifetime access". Plain digital file only. In this niche,
> offering to "review your documents" isn't just an Etsy Services violation — it edges
> toward practising law.
