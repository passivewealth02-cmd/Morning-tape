## 5. PRICING & RANK BOOSTERS

> ⚠ **Etsy Services-policy safety:** every SKU below is the **same digital files** at a
> different bundle — never a service. Do **not** offer setup help, consultations,
> **coaching, mentoring or "become a signing agent" training**, or "free updates /
> lifetime access" in the listing. The NSA space is *saturated* with coaching and course
> offers, and that is precisely the category Etsy removes — yours must stay a plain
> digital file. Do not offer notarial services through the listing either; you are selling
> a spreadsheet.

| SKU | What the buyer downloads | Price |
| --- | ------ | ----- |
| NOT-GS   | The Google Sheets / Excel file only | $19 |
| NOT-PDF  | The printable PDF only | $17 |
| NOT-BUNDLE | The spreadsheet + the printable PDF (both files) | **$25** |
| NOT-COMM | The same files, with a commercial-use file license | $45 |

- **Priced lowest in the business list on purpose — this is the volume play.** The NSA
  audience is large, active and buys tools constantly, but a lot of them are in their
  first year and price-sensitive. Expect this to move more units than the contractor or
  trucking products despite the smaller ticket.
- **Demand spikes whenever mortgage rates drop** — refi waves push thousands of new agents
  into the market at once — and again every **January** for tax season and the new mileage
  rate.
- Use all 10 photos + a walkthrough video that shows the *file* (not you offering
  training). Lead photo = the feature-forward hero; **the real-hourly engine is your single
  most persuasive image** — "$138.89 an hour is really $44.14 an hour" is a genuinely
  uncomfortable fact for this audience, and uncomfortable facts convert.
- **Say "check your state's maximum fee" and "this doesn't replace a bound journal"
  plainly.** Both are true, both protect you, and experienced notaries will trust the
  whole listing more because you said them.
- Cross-sell the **Bookkeeping & Tax** template — same Schedule C filer, same January
  panic. A "Signing Agent Bundle" of both at $49 is an easy upsell. It's fine to answer
  buyer questions in Messages after a sale, but never advertise support, setup, training,
  or updates as part of the listing.
