# Restaurant Command Center™ — Build Instructions

---

## A. Excel build

Requirements: Python 3.10+, `openpyxl >= 3.1.5`.

```bash
cd products/restaurant-command-center/build
python3 build_xlsx.py      # -> ../Restaurant_Command_Center.xlsx  (22-tab system + Welcome)
```

### Verifying
1. **Welcome** shows the intro + a Start-Here quick guide.
2. **Restaurant Dashboard** fills 12 KPI cards + 4 charts (cost structure,
   weekly sales, operations health, prime cost).
3. **Menu & Recipe Costing** — food cost % & margin per dish (enter price &
   cost, the rest calculates); **Sales Tracker** totals ~**$131,510** across
   30 days and **4,335** covers (avg check **$30.34**).
4. **P&L** — Food **32.1%**, Labor **32.8%**, Prime **64.9%**, Net Profit
   **$19,526** (14.8% margin). **Inventory** values **$7,945** with **7**
   low-stock flags.
5. **Reviews** average **4.6**; **Analytics** blends an **Operations Health
   Score of ~91%**. No broken cells.

> Note: uses `COUNTIF`, `COUNTA`, `AVERAGE`, `SUM` and ratio formulas — opens in
> Excel 2019/365 or Google Sheets. Sample sales span the last 30 days.

## F. Maintenance

- Edit sample data in `build_xlsx.py` and rerun; revenue, food/labor/prime
  cost %, net profit, inventory value, low-stock flags, ratings and the Health
  Score recompute automatically. Daily sales are generated by weekday pattern.
- Brand styles, dropdowns, KPI cards & summary tables are centralized.
- New sheet: add a builder and slot it into the `order` list in `main()`.
- Marketing numbers in `build_marketing.py` (`KPIS` list + content functions)
  should be kept in sync with the workbook's sample data.
