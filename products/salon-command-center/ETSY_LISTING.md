## 5. PRICING & RANK BOOSTERS

> ⚠ **Etsy Services-policy safety:** every SKU below is the **same digital files**
> at a different bundle — never a service. Do **not** offer setup help, custom pricing
> work, consultations, **coaching or mentoring**, "done-for-you" menu builds, or
> "free updates / lifetime access" in the listing. The beauty-business niche is packed
> with coaching offers — yours must stay a plain digital file or Etsy will pull it.

| SKU | What the buyer downloads | Price |
| --- | ------ | ----- |
| SAL-GS   | The Google Sheets / Excel file only | $22 |
| SAL-PDF  | The printable PDF only | $19 |
| SAL-BUNDLE | The spreadsheet + the printable PDF (both files) | **$29** |
| SAL-COMM | The same files, with a commercial-use file license (use it in your own salon) | $49 |

- **A huge, already-buying Etsy audience.** Stylists buy salon printables on Etsy in
  volume — client cards, formula cards, consultation forms. This is that same buyer with
  a real business problem attached, which is why $29 clears easily above the $5 printable
  crowd. The consultation form and colour formula card are *included*, so you're also
  competing on quantity.
- **Demand peaks in January** (new year, new prices) with a second lift in
  August–September when booth leases reset. List well before both.
- Use all 10 photos + a walkthrough video that shows the *file* (not you offering help).
  Lead photo = the feature-forward hero; **the menu-costed-by-the-hour image is your
  single most persuasive one** — a $35 cut out-earning a $95 colour is the exact fact
  that makes a stylist buy.
- **Say "card rates vary by processor" plainly.** It's honest and it stops "the fee was
  wrong" messages.
- Cross-sell the **Bookkeeping & Tax** and **Maker Pricing** templates — same
  self-employed buyer. A "Solo Business Bundle" of all three at $69 is an easy upsell.
  It's fine to answer buyer questions in Messages after a sale, but never advertise
  support, setup, or updates as part of the listing.
