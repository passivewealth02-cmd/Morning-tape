## 5. PRICING & RANK BOOSTERS

> ⚠ **Etsy Services-policy safety:** every SKU below is the **same digital files**
> at a different bundle — never a service. Do **not** offer setup help, custom
> rate-setting, licensing consultations, **coaching or mentoring**, "start your daycare
> with me" programs, or "free updates / lifetime access" in the listing. This niche is
> saturated with childcare-business coaching — yours must stay a plain digital file or
> Etsy will pull it.

| SKU | What the buyer downloads | Price |
| --- | ------ | ----- |
| DAY-GS   | The Google Sheets / Excel file only | $24 |
| DAY-PDF  | The printable PDF only | $22 |
| DAY-BUNDLE | The spreadsheet + the printable PDF (both files) | **$32** |
| DAY-COMM | The same files, with a commercial-use file license (use it in your own program) | $55 |

- **You are competing with $5 printable packs — and beating them twice.** Daycare
  enrollment forms, daily sheets and emergency cards sell in enormous volume on Etsy for
  $5–$12. All three are **included** here, so you win on quantity, and then you offer the
  thing none of them do: the business maths. That's what carries $32.
- **Demand peaks August–September** (new program year, new enrolments) with a second lift
  in **January** (rate increases and tax season). Have it listed by mid-July.
- Use all 10 photos + a walkthrough video that shows the *file* (not you offering help).
  Lead photo = the feature-forward hero; **the rate & break-even image is your single most
  persuasive one** — "five children only pay the bills, and one empty spot costs $10,846 a
  year" is the exact fact that makes a provider buy.
- **Say "ratios and CACFP tiers vary by state" plainly.** It's honest, it protects you,
  and licensed providers respect that you know the difference.
- Cross-sell the **Preschool Command Center** — it's curriculum and themes, this is the
  business, and the same provider needs both with zero overlap. A "Provider Bundle" of
  both at $45 is the easiest upsell in the shop. Also cross-sell **Bookkeeping & Tax**.
  It's fine to answer buyer questions in Messages after a sale, but never advertise
  support, setup, or updates as part of the listing.
